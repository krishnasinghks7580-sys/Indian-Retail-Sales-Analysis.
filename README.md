## This project focuses on analyzing Indian retail sales data using PostgreSQL and Power BI. 
## The objective is to identify business trends, analyze sales performance, evaluate profitability, and generate meaningful business insights through data analysis and visualization.
--Perform data exploration and data cleaning.
--Analyze retail sales and profitability.
--Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
--Evaluate sales performance and business growth.
--Generate actionable business insights.
--Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.
